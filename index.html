Focus Areas

Research Interests

⛓

Blockchain Security

Investigating DeFi manipulation, NFT wash trading, sniper bots, meme coin fraud, and smart contract vulnerabilities across Ethereum, BSC, and Solana.

🌐

Social Network Analysis

Detecting fake engagement, clone channels, coordinated inauthentic behavior, and conspiracy monetization on platforms like Telegram.

🔐

Cryptography & Privacy

Designing privacy-preserving protocols and accountability mechanisms for Bitcoin transactions, including stealth addresses and attribute-driven accountability.

🍯

Cyber Deception

Building honeypot systems, honeywords, and AI-driven deception techniques to defend against IP theft and advanced cyber threats.

🤖

AI for Security

Applying machine learning and deep learning to threat detection, fraud identification, and anomaly detection in large-scale online systems.

📊

Open Science & Datasets

Curating and publishing large-scale datasets (TGDataset: 120k+ channels, 400M+ messages) to enable reproducible security research.
